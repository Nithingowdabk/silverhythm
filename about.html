Founder's Note
Vandana Priya
Architect · Founder, Silverhythm · Bengaluru

As an architect, I have spent my career understanding how spaces speak — how proportion, material, and light can quietly shape the way a person feels. That understanding never left me when I walked into a room with a silver god photograph on the wall. I noticed that so many of these frames, however sacred their subject, were made without real design intent. The silver was there. The devotion was there. But the thoughtfulness — the sense that every element had been considered — was often missing. That gap is what Silverhythm was created to fill.

I grew up watching my father run his manufacturing unit with a quiet obsessiveness about quality. Decades of experience, passed down through every mould, every polish, every piece that left his workshop. When I brought my architectural training into that process, something shifted. The objects we began to create were different — rooted in tradition but shaped by design.

At Silverhythm, devotion is not just expressed — it is designed. Every frame is considered from the first sketch to the final finish. We ask: does this serve the deity it holds? Does it elevate the space it enters? Does it feel worthy of being passed down? Over the past ten years, I have had the privilege of seeing our pieces find their place in homes, mandirs, and hearts across Bengaluru and beyond. Each one is a reminder of why this work matters. Thank you for trusting us with your sacred spaces. We do not take that lightly.

— Vandana Priya
Founder, Silverhythm
